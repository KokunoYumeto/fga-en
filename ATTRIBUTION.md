# Sources and contributions

The original work is Alexandre Grothendieck’s *Fondements de la géométrie algébrique*: Bourbaki exposés 149, 182, 190, 195, 212, 221, 232 and 236, their errata, and the 1962 comments. French originals are available through [NUMDAM](https://www.numdam.org/) and the [Grothendieck Circle](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~leila.schneps/grothendieckcircle/FGA.pdf).

English wording shared with [Tim Hosgood’s human English translation](https://github.com/thosgood/fga) retains that translation’s attribution. The identified comparison version is [9976dd5f8e2ddcb388f0a07f3aa334f120d1019f](https://github.com/thosgood/fga/tree/9976dd5f8e2ddcb388f0a07f3aa334f120d1019f); this identifies the version compared, not a claim that every passage came from that version. The upstream translator’s note also acknowledges Steve Hnizdur for corrections and Jon Sterling for technical support. Those credits do not attribute unrelated AI-translated additions or AI-produced corrections to these contributors.

Additional English translation and subsequent corrections, editing and typesetting are AI-produced. The publisher did not translate the text. Attribution does not establish permission or endorsement.
