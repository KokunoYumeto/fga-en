\noindent\textbf{1. Duality theorem for coherent algebraic sheaves.}

\begin{center}
[Bourbaki Seminar, vol. 9, 1956/57, no. 149, 25 pp.]
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Remark on page 14.} --- As I pointed out in my lecture at the
International Congress of Mathematicians in 1958,\footnote{Grothendieck
(Alexander). --- The cohomology theory of abstract algebraic varieties,
\emph{Proceedings of the International Congress of Mathematicians}
[1958, Edinburgh], pp. 103--118. --- Cambridge, at the University Press, 1960.}
the questions raised here are now completely resolved.

The reader will find further information on the duality of coherent sheaves in
\emph{loco citato}\footnotemark[1], pp. 112--115, in EGA III, Part 2 (in
preparation), and, in the meantime, in SGA 1962. A more systematic treatment
will be found in a later chapter of EGA (Chapter IX in the planned outline).
