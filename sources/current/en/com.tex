\section*{Notice}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Notice}

We presented eight exposés in the Bourbaki Seminar, from 1957 to 1962,
concerning the foundations of algebraic geometry. Except for the first, these
exposés are written in the framework of schemes. All the results stated will
find their place in the “Éléments de géométrie algébrique” of Jean DIEUDONNÉ
and Alexander GROTHENDIECK. However, the substance of none of these exposés is
currently covered either by one of the chapters already published or in
preparation, or by any other book or article, and doubtless will not be for
some years yet. This is the main reason that led us to gather these exposés,
which will make available to users a certain number of key notions and results
of scheme theory while awaiting a definitive account. Moreover, reading these
texts will permit one to become familiar quickly with these results and
notions, without being impeded by the necessarily cumbersome detail of a
systematic treatise, and will provide the indispensable motivations for
studying such a treatise.

For the reader's convenience, we have gathered here a number of comments and
errata, grouped by exposé, which will in particular note in passing the
progress made since the text was written and indicate certain supplementary
references.

Various results appearing in these exposés have been treated in detail in the
IHES Seminar on Algebraic Geometry, as well as in two consecutive seminars at
Harvard in 1961/62, the first given by me and the second by MUMFORD--TATE, for
which mimeographed notes (written by Mr LICHTENBAUM) are currently in
preparation.
